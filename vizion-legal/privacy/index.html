Privacy Policy for VizionLight

Last updated: November 5, 2026

VizionLight (“the App”) helps users check whether a public Instagram profile currently has active stories and, after an optional in-app purchase, view those stories inside the App. We do not offer access to private accounts.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by Yaroslav Skachkov (Belarus). Contact: vizion.app.support@gmail.com

2. Data we process

  • Install identifier: a random ID stored on the device so we can improve reliability (for example pending purchases and analytics). It is not your name or email.
  • Usage events: actions such as app open, search, purchase result, errors (to operate and improve the service).
  • Search queries: Instagram usernames you enter, in order to perform the requested lookup.
  • Purchase data: handled by Apple In-App Purchase. We do not receive your full card number.
  • Device & app info: app version, iOS version, rough device model (diagnostics).
  • Crash / error information: if enabled, technical logs to fix bugs.

We do not require you to create an account with us.

3. How we use data

  • Provide story checks and story viewing features
  • Process unlocks and prevent abuse
  • Monitor service health and availability of our backend
  • Improve stability and user experience

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-party services

  • Apple – distribution, payments, device platform
  • Our hosting provider – runs the App’s API
  • Data providers – used only to fetch publicly available Instagram information you request

Their processing is governed by their own policies where applicable.

5. Instagram content

The App only works with content that is publicly available. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Instagram or Meta.

6. Retention

Operational logs and identifiers are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, security, and legal requirements, then deleted or aggregated.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your choices

You can stop using the App and delete it at any time. You may contact us at vizion.app.support@gmail.com about data that relates to your install identifier.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change; continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
