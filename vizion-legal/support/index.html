Common questions

  • The app shows “Maintenance” — our server may be temporarily unavailable. Please try again later.
  • No stories found — the profile may be private, or it currently has no active stories.
  • Purchase not working — make sure you are signed in with your Apple ID and try again. If the problem continues, contact us with your approximate purchase time.
